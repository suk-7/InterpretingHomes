Why Interpreting Homes

Healthcare that doesn't ask you to translate yourself.

01

Deaf-cultural fluency

We don't treat ASL as a translation problem. We treat Deaf culture as the context for care — because it is.

02

One team, integrated care

Behavioral health and nursing under one roof. No bouncing between providers who don't talk to each other.

03

In your home, in your community

Care happens where you live. Telehealth available when you prefer it.

04

Real clinical credentials

A registered nurse and a behavioral health technician with years in D/HOH-serving programs. Insurance-recognized scope.

How it works

Four steps from first message to first visit.

01

Reach out

Send a message, video call us, or have someone reach out for you. Tell us how you prefer to communicate.

02

We talk

A short conversation in ASL or English. We learn what you need. You learn whether we're the right fit.

03

We build a plan

Together we set goals and pick the services that match. We confirm coverage and pricing before you commit to anything.

04

We meet you

First visit happens at home, in your community, or by video — whichever works best for your situation.
