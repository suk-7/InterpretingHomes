About

Started by people who lived the gaps they're filling.

Interpreting Homes was founded by a registered nurse and a behavioral health technician who spent years watching Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing patients fall through cracks no one was assigned to catch.

We kept the name even though we're not interpreters — because the work is about interpretation in the broader sense: making the systems of healthcare make sense, in the language and culture of the people we serve.

Frequently asked

Questions we hear often.

Do I need to know ASL to work with you? +
No. We can provide support in ASL, spoken English, or both. Our team meets you in whatever communication mode works best for you.
Are you interpreters? +
No. We are not RID/BEI-credentialed interpreters and do not provide independent interpreting services. We offer ASL-informed health navigation — guidance and advocacy alongside your medical team, not in place of a qualified interpreter.
Do you take insurance or Medicaid? +
Payment options are confirmed when we connect so we can match you to the right service and funding source.
What does "ASL-informed" mean? +
It means our team has deep fluency in ASL and Deaf culture — not just basic signing. We understand the communication norms, the history, and the preferences of the community we serve, and we bring that into every interaction.
Where do you serve? +
In-person visits are available in Greater Columbus, Ohio. Telehealth services may be available across Ohio depending on the service type.
