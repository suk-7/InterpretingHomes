What we offer

Three core services. One integrated team.

A behavioral health technician with deep experience in the Deaf community and a registered nurse with broad clinical expertise — working together so you don't have to translate yourself across providers.

Behavioral health support

Trauma-informed mental health support, skill building, crisis help, and case management. Sessions in ASL or spoken English, in-home or in the community — wherever you feel safest.

Common reasons people come to us: anxiety, depression, post-trauma support, school or work stress, navigating life transitions, family communication conflict.

Nursing support

Care coordination, medication education, chronic-condition coaching, and post-hospital follow-up from a registered nurse who works in ASL-first communication.

Common reasons people come to us: diabetes management, blood pressure, recovering from surgery, understanding new prescriptions, preparing for a specialist appointment.

ASL-informed health navigation

Help finding the right doctor, understanding paperwork, preparing for appointments, and bridging the gaps the system leaves behind. Guidance and advocacy — not interpreting.

Important: we are not RID/BEI-credentialed interpreters and do not provide independent interpreting. We help you make sense of healthcare alongside your medical team.

Also available for organizations

Training and consulting for agencies. Sterile processing workforce development for D/HOH adults entering healthcare careers.
