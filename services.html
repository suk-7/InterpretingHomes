What we offer

Clinical care and the support that makes it work.

A licensed psychologist, a master's-prepared nurse, and an ASL-fluent care navigator — working together so you don't have to translate yourself across providers.

Primary clinical services

Individual psychotherapy

Telehealth · Statewide Ohio

Doctoral-level psychotherapy for anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, and life transitions. Delivered by a licensed psychologist via secure telehealth under PSYPACT, with an ASL mental-health interpreter when needed.

For: Adults and teens navigating mental health, recovery from trauma, family transitions, or co-occurring medical and emotional concerns.

Psychological assessments

Telehealth · Statewide Ohio

Comprehensive evaluations for SSDI disability claims, VA Compensation & Pension exams, cognitive screening, and fitness-for-duty. Administered and interpreted by a doctoral psychologist with two decades of assessment experience.

For: Adults seeking disability determinations, veterans navigating C&P, or families needing a thorough cognitive or psychological evaluation.

Skilled nursing & care coordination

In-home · Greater Columbus

Wound care, central-line and PICC management, medication education, post-hospital follow-up, and complex chronic-disease coordination — delivered by a master's-prepared RN with two decades in Ohio home health.

For: Adults recovering from surgery or hospitalization, managing diabetes or heart conditions, or coordinating care across multiple specialists.

Hospice & palliative care

In-home · Greater Columbus

End-of-life nursing care, symptom management, advance care planning, and grief support — for D/HOH patients and their families. Ohio's first integrated hospice service designed for the Deaf community.

For: Patients with serious illness, families facing end-of-life decisions, or anyone needing palliative support alongside curative care.

Supportive services

Behavioral health & skill building

In-home or Telehealth · ASL

Community Psychiatric Supportive Treatment (CPST), case management, crisis support, and skill building — delivered in ASL by a behavioral health specialist under clinical supervision. Wraps around therapy to make it work in real life.

For: People who need more than one therapy hour a week — help managing daily life, family relationships, school or work, or recovery between sessions.

ASL-informed health navigation

In-home or Telehealth · ASL

Help finding the right specialist, understanding paperwork, preparing for appointments, advocating across hearing providers, and bridging the gaps the system leaves behind. Guidance and advocacy — not independent interpreting.

Note: We are not RID or BEI-credentialed interpreters and do not provide independent interpreting. We help you make sense of healthcare alongside your medical team.

Also available for organizations

Training and consulting for agencies. Sterile processing workforce development for D/HOH adults entering healthcare careers.
